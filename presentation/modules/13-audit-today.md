# Module — Lekha-Jokha Register & Today Special

Two small screens that carry disproportionate weight in a presentation.
One proves **accountability**; the other proves **transparency**.

---

# PART A — 📝 LEKHA-JOKHA REGISTER (`/audit-log`)

**Priority: P0 — for any officer who has ever been asked "ye entry kisne
ki thi?", this is the whole product.**

**Who:** **Company Commander only.**

**Source:** `src/features/auditLog/screens/AuditLogScreen.tsx` (119 L),
`src/services/auditLog.service.ts` (53 L)

**Verification:** CONFIRMED.

## 1. The Pitch

> "Har badlav ka record. Kisne kiya, kab kiya, kya kiya, aur kyun kiya —
> saaf Hindi me likha hua."

## 2. What Gets Recorded

Every entry carries: **who** (name and role), **what action**, **which
register**, **which record**, **a plain-language description**, and an
**exact timestamp**.

Five action types: **Create · Update · Delete · Login · Export**.

Five live counters across the top: **Total · Creates · Updates · Deletes ·
Logins**, plus a filter dropdown and a free-text search.

## 3. ⭐ The Descriptions Are Written For Humans

This is the detail that lands. Audit entries are not machine codes — they
are readable Hindi sentences. A real example generated by the trainee
report approval:

> *"045 Ramesh Kumar ki 'Bukhar' report approve ki (S · 2026-09-04 se
> 2026-09-06, 3 din). Wajah: tez bukhar. Absent register + MI register +
> notice board update hua."*

**What to say — this is the line:**
> "Dekhiye ye entry. Ye kisi programmer ke liye nahi likhi gayi. Ye us
> officer ke liye likhi hai jo chhe mahine baad poochega — ye kya hua tha?
>
> Naam hai, tareekh hai, kitne din hai, wajah hai, aur kaun se register
> badle wo bhi likha hai. Ek line padho, poori kahani samajh aa jaati hai."

## 4. Why A Buyer Cares

> "Jab inspection aata hai, ya koi shikayat hoti hai, ya court case banta
> hai — sabse pehla sawaal yahi hota hai: ye entry kisne ki thi aur kab.
> Register me signature dhoondhna padta hai, aur signature kabhi mil bhi
> nahi paata.
>
> Yahan ek search box hai. Naam daalo, sab kuch saamne."

## 5. Empty-State Polish

When nothing matches, the screen says **"Koi audit log nahi"** in Hindi
with a clean icon — not a blank page.

## 6. ⚠️ Do NOT Promise

- ❌ **"Every action in the system is audited."** **PARTIAL — be honest.**
  There are **three separate logging systems** in the codebase:
  `auditLogs` (what `/audit-log` reads), `staff_activity_logs` (written
  from ~18 ustad screens, **no UI reads it**), and `activity_logs`
  (orphan). Coverage of `auditLogs` is **good for trainee reports, files
  and leave**, but it is **not universal**.
  **Say:** *"Trainee reports, files aur leave ka poora record hai."*
  **Do not say:** *"Har cheez ka record hai."*
- ❌ **"Tamper-proof / immutable log."** Not verified.
- ❌ **"Export the audit log."** `Export` is an action *type* that can be
  logged; a verified export of the log itself was not traced.
- ❌ **"Unlimited history."** The service reads the most recent **200**
  entries by default.
- ❌ **"Other roles can see their own audit trail."** CC-only.

# PART B — 🌟 TODAY SPECIAL (`/today`)

**Priority: P1 — but it is the most *likeable* screen in the product.**

**Who:** **Everyone.** All staff roles **and trainees**. This is the only
substantial screen in FCOY that every single user can open.

**Source:** `src/features/activity/screens/TodaySpecialScreen.tsx`,
`src/features/activity/api/activity.api.ts`

**Verification:** CONFIRMED.

## 7. The Pitch

> "Aaj company me kya-kya hua — ek page par, sabke liye. Commander se lekar
> rangroot tak, sabko wahi sach dikhta hai."

## 8. Nine Activity Kinds

The feed pulls from **9 collections** and tags each event:

| Kind | Label as shown | Icon |
|---|---|---|
| movement | **Kaun kaha gaya** | 🚶 |
| medical | MI / Hospital | 🏥 |
| report | Reports | 📝 |
| notice | Notices | 📢 |
| file | Files | 📁 |
| training | Training | 🎯 |
| staff | Staff | 👮 |
| relegation | Relegation | ⚠️ |
| admin | Admin | ⚙️ |

Each kind has its own colour stripe. One-tap filters, plus a search across
title, detail, reason, actor and subject.

**Point at the label "Kaun kaha gaya."** It is not "Movement Register" — it
is the question a person actually asks.

## 9. ⭐ The Daily Digest — written in Hindi

The top of the screen summarises the day in plain sentences, generated from
the real counts:

> 🏥 3 medical entry (MI room / hospital)
> 🚶 5 trainee away hue (chutti / absent)
> 📝 2 nayi report aayi
> 📢 1 notice publish hua

And when nothing happened:

> 😴 **Aaj koi badi activity record nahi hui**

**What to say:**
> "Ye headline system ne khud likhi hai — aaj ke asli data se. Aur agar
> aaj kuch nahi hua, to wo bhi imaandari se likha hai: 'aaj koi badi
> activity record nahi hui'. Khaali screen dikhane ke bajaye."

That empty-state sentence is worth pointing at. It shows care.

## 10. ⭐ Everyone Sees The Same Day

**This is the reason to demo it.**

> "Sabse badi baat — ye page rangroot bhi dekh sakta hai. Commander bhi
> wahi dekhta hai, clerk bhi, aur rangroot bhi.
>
> Company me afwah tab failti hai jab kisi ko pata nahi hota ki hua kya
> hai. Yahan sab ek hi page dekh rahe hain."

The screen also supports an **embedded mode**, so the same feed appears
inside the trainee dashboard as a tab.

## 11. Demo Script (45 seconds)

1. As Commander, open `/today`. Read the Hindi digest aloud.
2. Tap the **🚶 Kaun kaha gaya** filter. "Aaj kaun kahan gaya."
3. Tap **🏥 MI / Hospital**.
4. **Now log out and log in as a trainee.** Open the Today Special tab.
   **Same feed.**
5. Land it: *"Ek hi sach, sabke liye. Ye sirf software ki baat nahi — ye
   company chalane ka tareeka hai."*

## 12. ⚠️ Do NOT Promise

- ❌ **"Trainees see everything."** They see the feed; sensitive registers
  remain role-locked.
- ❌ **"Live / real-time streaming feed."** It loads for a chosen date.
- ❌ **"Historical feed for any past date going back years."** Query is by
  batch and date; deep history was not verified.
- ⚠️ **Demo caveat:** trainee-visible data depends on deployed Firestore
  rules. If the rules have not been deployed, this screen can look empty
  for a trainee. **Check it before the demo.**
